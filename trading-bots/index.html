AFT Trading Bots

Automated futures strategies for TradingView, built on the All Fluence method.

The AFT trading bots are rule-based strategies that run in TradingView on the 1-minute chart. They are built on the same concepts taught in the All Fluence course and used by the Master Trader indicators: market structure, imbalances and session timing. Every entry, stop and target is decided by fixed rules on closed bars, and nothing repaints.

Each strategy can run on its own as a TradingView strategy, and it can also send its orders to your own futures broker through a third-party automation service you set up and control.

How it will work

From access to your chart

  1. Access on TradingView

    Each strategy is an invite-only script on TradingView. Access is granted to your TradingView username.

  2. Read it before you run it

    Every strategy's TradingView page explains what it trades, when it trades, how it exits and what the Strategy Tester assumes for costs.

  3. Paper first, then your call

    Run it in TradingView's Strategy Tester or on a paper account first. You choose the contract size, and you decide whether to connect it to a broker at all.
