    Every strategy is shown the same way

    The same parts in the same order, so you can put any two side by side.

    1. What it is

      The market, the timeframe, the session, and what it trades in one sentence.

    2. The label

      Every figure sits under the words Hypothetical backtest. A backtest is not a trading result.

    3. The four figures

      Net profit, win rate, profit factor and max drawdown, exactly as TradingView's Strategy Tester reports them.

    4. The pictures

      The equity curve day by day and every month on a grid. Losing months show as plainly as winning ones.

    5. The test

      The window, the starting balance, the order size, and the commission and slippage assumed.

    6. The proof

      The date the test was run, so you can see how current the record is.

    A strategy with no published record says so. No number is estimated, and none is carried over from another strategy.

    From checkout to your chart

    1. Access on TradingView

      Each strategy is an invite-only script on TradingView. Access is granted to your TradingView username.

    2. Read it before you run it

      Every strategy's TradingView page explains what it trades, when it trades, how it exits and what the Strategy Tester assumes for costs.

    3. Paper first, then your call

      Run it in TradingView's Strategy Tester or on a paper account first. You choose the contract size, and you decide whether to connect it to a broker at all.
