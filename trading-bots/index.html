          Every strategy is shown the same way

          The same parts in the same order, so you can put any two side by side.

          1. The strategy

            The market, the timeframe and its plan.

          2. The label

            Each strategy's full record carries the words Hypothetical backtest. A backtest is not a trading result.

          3. The figures

            On its card: return on drawdown, average monthly profit, win rate, total profit, max drawdown and profit factor. In its full record: net profit, win rate, profit factor and max drawdown, exactly as TradingView's Strategy Tester reports them.

          4. The pictures

            The equity curve day by day and every month on a grid. Losing months show as plainly as winning ones.

          5. The test

            The window, the starting balance, and the commission and slippage assumed.

          6. The proof

            The date the test was run, so you can see how current the record is.

          A strategy with no published record says so. No number is estimated, and none is carried over from another strategy.

          From checkout to your chart

          1. Access on TradingView

            Each strategy is an invite-only script on TradingView. Claim your access through Whop with your TradingView username (how).

          What do I need to run a bot on my own account?

          Three things from other companies, separate from your plan and paid to those companies: a TradingView account on a paid plan with real-time futures data, a CrossTrade account, which carries the orders from TradingView to your account, and a Tradovate account or an account at a prop firm CrossTrade connects to (demo, prop or live). Some prop firms do not allow automated trading: check your firm's rules before you buy. The set-up guide lists each one with its link.
