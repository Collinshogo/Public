How to read it

  • The strategies are ranked by RoDD, so a place reflects profit against risk, not dollars alone. A month's RoDD is that month's profit divided by the strategy's max drawdown over its whole record (the drawdown its card states), shown as a percent.
  • The dollar figure on each line is the month's profit: that month's line in the strategy's Monthly P&L table, on its card in the Strategy Library.
  • A month's figure counts the trades closed in that month and no others. A trade closed at or after 6:00 p.m. New York time counts toward the next weekday, the futures session it belongs to.
  • The current month comes first, on the left, and the months before it follow, up to twelve in all. When a new month starts it takes the first place, and once there are twelve the oldest drops off.
  • The latest month is still in progress. Each record stops on its last tested day, shown on its line, so the strategies are not all measured to the same day.
  • In each month the bars are drawn against that month's highest RoDD, so they compare the strategies within a month, not one month with another. A loss is red and carries a minus sign.
  • The small mark under a place shows how many places the strategy moved from the month before.
  • A strategy marked Coming soon is not released yet.
  • A place here is a place in a list of hypothetical results. It does not say what a strategy will do next.
