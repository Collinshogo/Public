      How this is worked out

      Every traded day is a start: the replay runs the strategy's tested days from each one, in order. Each day is replayed along its own ups and downs, from one turning point to the next. The test does not record whether a trade's best or worst point came first. The main result takes the worst point first in a trade that closed at a profit and the best point first in any other trade. The second result takes the best point first in every trade. Without a consistency rule, the second result is never the higher of the two. With a consistency rule on, it can be. Where trades overlap, they are taken one after another in the order they closed.

      The loss limit is Static, a Trailing drawdown or an End-of-day drawdown, as chosen above. A daily loss limit stops that day at the limit, and a consistency rule stops a day at its share of the target; the account trades again the next day.

      The success rate counts the starts that finished: those that reached the target and those that hit the loss limit. Starts that did not finish before the test ended are not counted in the rate. The account's own fees are not counted; commission and slippage are as in the test. Rules differ from account to account, so type your own.
