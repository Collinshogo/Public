Before you start

What you need, and two words to know

This guide adds one CrossTrade feature to a bot that is already running: the trade copier. It runs on CrossTrade's servers, so your computer can be off.

Leader
The account your bot trades: the one named in the strategy's Tradovate account setting. The bot's alert sends its orders here, and only here.
Follower
Any other account. CrossTrade trades it for you by repeating what the leader does. You never point the bot or an alert at a follower.
Fill
An order that has been carried out. "The leader fills" means its order went through.
Market order
An order that is carried out at once, at the current price.

Accounts under different logins

CrossTrade's help page says what it has validated: a leader and followers under the same Tradovate login and of the same kind (Demo to Demo, or Live to Live).

Copying between two different logins (two prop firm logins, or a demo login and a prop firm login), or between Live and Demo, it calls "still being validated", and it tells you to check the behaviour with demo accounts before relying on it.

  • All your accounts under one login: carry on with step 1.
  • Accounts under different logins: you can still set the copier up the same way. Treat the first trades as a test: keep the bot's Multiplier at 1, watch the first trade on every account, and read the copier's log after every session. If a follower does not copy correctly, switch the copier off and write to CrossTrade's support. Its address is under Troubleshooting, at the end of this page.
  • One demo account and one prop firm account: those are two logins, so there is no all-demo practice run for you. Go ahead only if both of these are true: your prop firm's rules allow automated trading and copying between accounts, and you accept that the first copied trades are real trades on the prop firm account. If either is not true, stop here and keep the bot on one account.
  • Which account is the leader: the one the bot's alert trades today. The other account is the follower. If the bot is still on your demo practice account and you want it on another account, move it first: Moving the bot to another account, in the bot setup guide.
  • Only one account so far: there is nothing to copy to yet. The optional monitor further down still works on one account; apart from that you are done with this guide for now. A Tradovate sign-up opens with one demo account. A second account usually comes from a prop firm. To ask whether your Tradovate login can have another demo account, use the support chat in Tradovate's Help Center.

Set up the copier

Watch this part (0:33)

Before you click anything: check that your prop firm allows copying between accounts, and if your accounts sit under different logins, read the box above.

  • Connect every account you want to use to CrossTrade: My Account, then Brokers, then Link Demo or Link Live.It is the same as step 2 of the bot setup guide. Each Tradovate login is linked once, and all of its accounts then appear under it.
  • Wait until the bot holds no trade. Between 16:09 and 18:00 New York time (US Eastern time) is simplest.To check, look at the leader account in Tradovate's web app: no position and no orders waiting. On a normal trading day the bot holds no trade between those two times.A new copier copies what the leader does from then on. It does not pick up a trade that is already open.
  • In CrossTrade's sidebar, open Tradovate Copier.
  • Click Add Copier.
  • Give the copier a name if you like.
  • Under Account Selection, pick the leader: the account your bot trades.
  • Pick the follower account, or several.You can pick several followers in one copier. They then share one set of settings. If two followers should trade different sizes, give each its own copier instead. The same leader and follower cannot be put in a second copier.
  • Under Trade Execution, change the mode from Signal to Execution. It starts on Signal, so do not skip this line.In Execution mode, every fill on the leader is repeated on the followers as a market order. Step 2 explains what that means for you. Signal, the mode it starts on, repeats the messages sent to the leader instead of its fills. This guide does not use it.The form also offers a third mode, Order. This guide does not use it.
  • Under Sizing, leave the ratio at 1x to copy the leader's size.The ratio is how many contracts the follower trades for each one the leader trades. 2 doubles it and 0.5 halves it. If a follower is a prop firm account, keep its size inside that account's contract limit.
  • Leave Max Position Size at 0, which means no limit.It is an optional ceiling on the follower's position, counted in contracts. If you do set one, make it at least the largest position your bot takes on the leader. A lower number trims or skips the follower's entries.
  • Under Instrument Filtering, pick the instrument your bot trades: MNQ for Slipstream, MGC for Midas.Anything else on the leader is then not copied. This is the instrument's root, not a dated contract code, so it stays the same when the contract month changes.
  • Scroll down to Account Settings. Leave Account Management Tandem Mode off for now.It only matters if you add the optional monitor later. Monitor step 2 tells you when to switch it on. Leave Stealth Mode, in the same block, as it is: it only changes the label on copied orders.
  • Under Safety Features, leave Auto-Sync enabled.After each copy it checks that the follower matches the leader.
  • Leave every other setting as it comes.
  • Before you click the button in the next line, know this: a follower has no stop and no targets of its own at the broker. It gets in when the leader gets in, and gets out when the leader gets out.Step 2 has the rest of what Execution mode means for a follower.
  • Click Create Copier & Enable, the blue button at the bottom of the window.The button beside it, Create Copier, saves the copier switched off.

You should now see: the copier in the list on the Tradovate Copier page, enabled, in Execution mode.

Leave the bot's alert exactly as it is

Nothing changes in TradingView. The bot keeps its one alert, aimed at the leader. Never create an alert, or a second copy of the strategy, for an account that is a follower: it would take every trade twice.

Trade copier · step 2 of 3

What Execution mode means for a follower

Watch this part (1:48)

Read this before the first copied trade. There is nothing to click here, but the first line can cost you money if you miss it.

  • Do not close a follower by hand in the middle of a trade. Auto-Sync can put it back to match the leader. To take a follower out, pause its copier first (switch it off in the list on the Tradovate Copier page), then close that account's position yourself in Tradovate.
  • A follower has no stop and no targets of its own at the broker. It gets in when the leader gets in, and gets out when the leader gets out.
  • Pausing a copier does not close anything. Positions and orders stay where they are until you close them.
  • A follower's prices differ a little from the leader's. It enters and exits at market just after the leader fills.
  • Fills that happen while the connection is down are not copied afterwards. Look at every account after any outage.
  • Each account succeeds or fails separately. A follower that is rejected does not undo the leader's trade.
  • Unlinking a Tradovate login pauses every copier and every monitor. Linking it again does not switch them back on: open both pages and enable them again.

Trade copier · step 3 of 3

Check it is copying

Watch this part (2:08)

  • Wait for the bot's next trade. That can take hours, and there is nothing to see until then. You can close the browser: the copier runs on CrossTrade's servers. Come back when the leader is in a trade.
  • In Tradovate's web app, look at each follower's position while the leader is in the trade.
  • On CrossTrade's Tradovate Copier page, click See Logs, in the toolbar at the top beside Add Copier.The log has a row for every copy, and the reason for anything that was skipped or rejected.
  • When the leader exits, check each follower again: it should hold no position.

You should now see: each follower holding the same position as the leader, at the size you set, and a row for each follower in the log.

The test is passed when each follower took the same trade as the leader at the size you set, the log has a row for it with nothing skipped or rejected, and every account holds no position after the leader exits. If any of those fails, switch the copier off and see Troubleshooting.

Read the log after every session for the first week. If you can, do this practice run on demo accounts before you use real ones.

Moving from the practice run to other accounts
  1. Wait until the bot holds no trade. Between 16:09 and 18:00 New York time is simplest.
  2. On the Tradovate Copier page, switch the practice copier off. It can stay in the list, switched off.
  3. Make a new copier the same way (step 1), with the new leader and followers.
  4. If the leader is a different account from the one the bot trades now, the bot has to trade the new leader. Put the new account name in the strategy's Tradovate account setting, then delete the alert and create it again (steps 5 and 6 of the bot setup guide).

Optional

Put a monitor on an account

You do not need a monitor to run the bot or the copier. A monitor is an optional CrossTrade feature that puts fixed rules on one account, such as a daily loss limit, a daily profit target or a time of day to hold no position. When a rule is hit, the monitor can close the account's position and block the bot's orders on that account until the monitor is started again. Like the copier, it is part of CrossTrade's Pro plan and higher.

Add a monitor and set daily limits

Watch this part (0:25)

Steps 1 and 2 fill in one window. Nothing is saved until you click the button at the end of step 2, so do both in one go.

  • In CrossTrade's sidebar, open Tradovate Account Manager.
  • Click Add Monitor.
  • Under Account Selection, pick the account you want the rules on.Each account takes one monitor. For rules on several accounts, do steps 1 and 2 once for each account. If you use monitors with a copier, give the leader and each follower its own.
  • Leave Monitor Mode on Standard Mode.The monitor then stops for the day when a daily limit is hit.
  • Under Daily P/L Thresholds, type your Daily Profit Target and your Daily Loss Limit.Each is an amount of profit or loss for the whole day, in your account's currency. A limit left at 0 is switched off. The amounts are yours to choose: take them from your own account's rules and from what you are prepared to lose in a day. A prop firm states its daily loss limit, if it has one, in the rules for your account: type a smaller amount than that.CrossTrade's help page does not show an example amount. Type the loss limit as a plain amount, then prove it on a demo account with the test at the end of step 3 before you rely on it.
  • Leave P/L Basis on Total.Total counts open trades as well as closed ones, so it sees a losing trade while it is still open.

You should now see: your two amounts in the Daily P/L Thresholds boxes. The monitor is not saved yet. Keep the window open and go on to step 2.

Monitor · step 2 of 3

The optional rules

Watch this part (0:54)

Everything below the daily limits is optional. If you want none of it, skip the fold-out and click Create Monitor & Enable, the blue button at the bottom of the window.

What each optional rule does
  • Account Floor: a fixed minimum account value. Below it the monitor cancels working orders, closes the position and blocks the bot's orders. It does not move with your prop firm's own threshold.
  • Minimum Profit: once the day is up by an amount you set, it stops the day falling back below a level you choose.
  • Trailing Drawdown: follows the session's high and stops the account if it gives back more than your amount.
  • Position Management and Order Management: what happens when a rule is hit. As they come, the account's position is closed (Flatten account) and further messages are blocked (Disable alerts). Leave both as they come.
  • End-of-Day Flattening: closes the account's position at a time you choose, in Eastern time. The bot already closes everything at 16:09 New York time, so this is a second line only. If you use it, pick a time just after that, for example 16:10.
  • News Lockout: can close the position ahead of major US news and block the bot's orders around it. Leave it off unless you want it: blocking the bot part-way through a trade leaves the account out of step with the chart.
  • Entry Limits and Discipline Rules (both marked BETA): a cap on size, a limit on trades per session, a stop after a run of losses. All off as they come.
  • Trading Windows: limit the hours the account may trade. Leave these alone, for the same reason as News Lockout.
  • When your rules are in, click Create Monitor & Enable, the blue button at the bottom of the window.The button beside it, Create Monitor, saves the monitor switched off.
  • If this account is a follower in a copier, turn on Tandem Mode in that copier. On the Tradovate Copier page open the copier for editing (the window is titled Edit Copier), scroll to Account Settings, switch on Account Management Tandem Mode and save.The follower is then copied to only while its own monitor is active. Without it, a follower whose monitor has stopped is still copied to.

You should now see: the monitor in the list, reading ACTIVE, with no warning beside it.

ACTIVE means switched on and watching the account. PAUSED means you switched it off. STOPPED means a limit was reached and the monitor stopped for the day.

Monitor · step 3 of 3

When a monitor stops an account

Watch this part (1:51)

  • CrossTrade blocks the bot's orders for that account until you start the monitor again. CrossTrade's help says a stopped monitor resumes when you restart it, so do not count on it starting again by itself.
  • The bot on your chart does not know, and keeps running. For the rest of that day the chart and the account can differ. That is expected.
  • When a follower's monitor stops, that follower's position is closed and, with Tandem Mode on, it is skipped until its monitor is active again. The leader and the other followers carry on.
  • A monitor is not a stop order and it does not guarantee a maximum loss. It acts after a limit is crossed, so price can move further first.
  • It does not copy your prop firm's drawdown calculation. Your firm's own figure is the one that counts. Keep your limits inside it.
  • It stops CrossTrade, not you. A trade you click inside Tradovate is not blocked in advance.
  • To start again: check the account in Tradovate first.
  • Wait until the strategy on the chart is flat. Between 16:09 and 18:00 New York time is the simplest moment.
  • On the Tradovate Account Manager page, change the limits if needed and restart the monitor.The two small view buttons in the toolbar, beside See Logs, switch the page between a list and cards. In the card view, the stopped account's card has a restart button.

You should now see: the monitor reading ACTIVE again.

Test it on a demo account first: set a small limit, let it be reached, and see for yourself what the monitor does.

If something is wrong

Troubleshooting

The leader traded and a follower did nothing

Click See Logs and find that follower and that time. Then check, in this order:

  • The copier is enabled, and its leader is the account the bot trades.
  • The follower's Tradovate link has not expired (My Account, Brokers).
  • The leader's order has actually filled. In Execution mode the copy happens at the fill, not when the order is placed.
  • Tandem Mode is on and the follower's monitor is STOPPED or PAUSED.
  • The instrument filter does not match the contract, or Max Position Size is too low for the entry.
  • The log shows a rejection from the broker.

If there is no row at all, send CrossTrade's support the mode, the leader, the follower, the contract and the time.

A follower reopened after I closed it

Auto-Sync put it back to match the leader, which was still in the trade. Pause that follower's copier first, then close the account's position. Check the position and the working orders afterwards: pausing does not close anything.

A follower holds a different size from the leader
  • Its ratio is not 1x.
  • Max Position Size trimmed the entry. The log says so.
  • A copy was skipped or rejected part-way through a trade. With Auto-Sync on, CrossTrade corrects it.
A follower took the same trade twice

It is receiving the entry by two routes: the copier, and an alert or a second copy of the strategy aimed at it directly. Stop and delete the extra alert while the strategy is flat, close the position in Tradovate, and keep one alert for the leader only.

The log says a copy was skipped because the order could not be sent

The order never reached Tradovate. The usual cause is Tradovate's limit on how many requests one login may send. Every follower on a login adds to that count. If skips come in busy sessions, put fewer followers on one login.

A monitor says STOPPED

One of its limits was reached today. See when a monitor stops an account for what that means and how to start again.

Everything paused after I linked a login again

Unlinking any Tradovate login pauses all copiers and all monitors, and linking again does not turn them back on. Open Tradovate Copier and Tradovate Account Manager, review each one and enable it again, while the strategy is flat.
