The method

Enter where the money enters.

"Breakout trading is for gamblers. Do not enter before the liquidity sweep or you become the liquidity."
shogotrades, from the course

  1. Bias from the higher timeframe

    The 1-minute never overrules the 4-hour.

  2. Wait for the pullback into the stack

    Imbalance midline, 2H/4H level, VWAP band, opening-range edge. More in line, higher the grade.

  3. Rest the order. Tight structural stop.

    Limit at the level, stop behind the structure that would prove you wrong. Size from ATR.

  4. Target structure. Take what's given.

    Next level, next imbalance. "Don't be a dick for a tick."
