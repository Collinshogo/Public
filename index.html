The indicators

Two Master Traders, one per instrument. One overlay underneath both.

Each Master Trader is tuned to how its market actually moves: Nasdaq runs and gold grinds, so they are not the same script with a different ticker. Both sit on top of The All Fluence indicator, the structure-and-levels overlay the course is taught on.

MNQ Master Trader

The MNQ playbook, live on your chart.

The MNQ trade book is a set of documented session setups: opening-range plays, imbalance pullbacks, the New York open, the overnight sessions. The MNQ Master Trader draws each one as it forms, so you see what the desk sees.

  • Each setup's entry level, stop and targets drawn when the conditions are met, labelled by setup name.
  • Stop management shown as it happens: the move to break-even, the reduced-risk stop, the add that fires on a confirming close.
  • Resting limit orders drawn where the playbook rests them, removed when they are cancelled.
  • Alert conditions for every event, so you are not glued to the screen waiting.
  • Trades confirm on closed bars. Nothing repaints after the fact.

MGC Master Trader

Gold has its own clock. This one keeps it.

Gold's moves cluster around London, the New York fix and the Asian sessions, and it respects imbalances differently from the Nasdaq. The MGC Master Trader carries the MGC trade book's setups with their own session windows and risk rules.

  • Session-anchored setups for the London open, the New York morning and the Asian hours, drawn in New York time.
  • Entry, stop and target levels per setup, with the same closed-bar confirmation as the MNQ version.
  • Stop moves, partials and cancellations shown on the chart, not buried in a log.
  • Alert conditions on every event.

How it's traded

Enter like the people who move the market, not like the people chasing it.

"Breakout trading is for gamblers. Do not enter before the liquidity sweep or you become the liquidity."

Collin · from the course

  1. Bias from the higher timeframe

    Which timeframe's imbalances are being made and which are being broken decides direction. The 1-minute never overrules the 4-hour.

  2. Wait for the pullback into the stack

    The disrespected imbalance's midline, a 2H/4H level, a VWAP band, an opening-range edge, a session open. The more that line up at one price, the higher the grade.

  3. Rest the order, tight structural stop

    Limit orders at the level, stop behind the structure that would prove the idea wrong. Size from the ATR readout, not from feeling.

  4. Target structure, take what's given

    The next level, the next imbalance up the ladder. No fixed dollar target. "Don't be a dick for a tick."

That is the whole method, and it is the same one Collin streams. The course makes each step concrete; the indicators make each step visible; the Discord is where you watch it done and ask why.
