# The AFT Challenge

**All Fluence Trading** — *Never let a green day turn red.*

The AFT Challenge is a structured consistency program for developing futures traders. It simulates the full prop-firm journey — from passing an evaluation all the way to your first payout — on a paper-trading account, so you build the discipline before you ever pay an evaluation fee.

The challenge trains the three skills that separate consistent traders from everyone else: **sizing appropriately, accepting losses before they spiral, and accepting profit.** There is no difference between this and trading in the prop space — except that this way costs you nothing.

> ### Your journey starts today
> This challenge isn't about home runs. It's about becoming the trader who shows up, takes their one or two best trades, banks the green, and walks away — **every single day, on command.** That skill is the entire business. Open your five paper accounts, set your loss limits, and take your first rep tomorrow morning. It costs you nothing — and it builds everything.

## Challenge Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Accounts | 5 × $2,000 | Simulated. Traded independently. |
| Daily loss limit | **−$200** | Per account, per day. Hard stop. |
| Profit goal | **+$5,500** | The typical prop-firm payout threshold — this run takes you from eval to payout. |
| Instruments | MNQ · MES · MGC | Micro Nasdaq, S&P, and Gold futures. |
| Max position | 10 micros | Scale in freely — but never so large that one trade can blow the daily loss limit. |

Use TradingView or any paper-trading platform, as long as it lets you set a daily loss limit. Prop-firm accounts are allowed, but paper is recommended — same conditions, zero cost.

## How the Challenge Is Traded

1. **Trade five accounts, each on its own.** No copy trading. Every account is traded separately, with its own decisions and its own P&L. You are not required to trade all five every day.
2. **The Profit Acceptance Rule.** The moment a closed trade puts an account over **+$100 on the day**, that account is finished until tomorrow. Move to your next account. This is the heart of the challenge: learn to accept profit.
3. **No cap on winning — only on greed.** There is no maximum win per day across your accounts. But the Profit Acceptance Rule always applies, account by account, with no exceptions.
4. **One to two trades per account, tops.** Quality over volume. You're building reps and sets — a small number of deliberate trades, repeated daily, is what grows consistency and proficiency.
5. **Two consecutive losses — stand down.** Two losses in a row on an account ends that account's day. The market is telling you something; listen from the sidelines.
6. **Respect the red zone.** If you've used $150 of your $200 daily loss limit, seriously consider not taking another trade. Living to trade tomorrow *is* winning.

> ### Why +$100 and done
> New traders don't blow up because they can't find winners — they blow up because they can't keep them. Banking a green account and walking away rewires the instinct that turns winning days into losing ones.
>
> And know what that $100 becomes at scale — the skill is identical, only the account count changes. **20 accounts × $100 is $2,000 a day. That's $40,000 a month**, built out of the "small" profits this rule teaches you to accept.

> ### ⚠️ The real objective: don't blow the account
> You will hit your daily loss limit some days — that's fine. Losses are part of this business, and taking them and walking away is exactly what you're here to learn. What you're protecting is the account itself, and do the math on how hard these rules make it to lose: at −$200 a day, blowing a $2,000 account takes **ten maximum-loss days in a row.** Ten.
>
> That's real risk management. Professionals risk 5% or less of their total account — while the prop space sets you up for failure with structures that put 10–30% of your real capital on the line in a single day. Learn to accept the loss, protect the account, and live to trade tomorrow, and you'll walk into the prop space already trading like a professional.
